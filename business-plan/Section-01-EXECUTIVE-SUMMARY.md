## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Company Overview

And Now Breathe is an early-stage digital health technology company developing an AI-powered, extended reality (XR) guided meditation application specifically designed to support mental health recovery, with initial focus on addiction recovery populations. The solution addresses a critical barrier in meditation practice: the inability to effectively visualize environments and objects during guided sessions, which affects approximately 2-5% of the general population (aphantasia) and potentially higher rates among those experiencing mental health challenges.

### The Opportunity

The UK digital mental health market is projected to reach £2.1 billion by 2027, with the NHS actively investing in evidence-based digital therapeutics. The addiction recovery market alone represents significant opportunity, with over 600,000 adults in contact with drug and alcohol services in England annually, and mental health services serving approximately 2 million people.

### Unique Value Proposition

And Now Breathe combines proven therapeutic techniques (leaves on a stream, balloon releases) with:

- **AI-personalized narrative generation** that integrates user inputs into spoken meditation
- **3D virtual environments** customizable to personal connections and memories
- **Graceful enhancement architecture** delivering core experiences from basic smartphones through to enterprise VR headsets
- **Evidence-based approach** targeting specific mental health challenges (attachment, rumination, cognitive distortion)

Unlike competitors (Headspace, Calm, Tripp), And Now Breathe specifically addresses visualization barriers through interactive 3D environments, targets clinical populations with evidence-gathering strategy, uses AI to create deeply personalized experiences, and employs graceful enhancement for maximum accessibility.

### Business Model - Primary Revenue Streams

1. **NHS/Healthcare B2B2C** (Priority) - Licensing to NHS trusts, IAPT services, addiction clinics
2. **Corporate Wellness** - Employee assistance programs, workplace mental health
3. **Consumer Freemium** - Free core experience, premium features subscription
4. **Research Partnerships** - University collaborations, clinical trial participation
5. **Licensing** - White-label for therapeutic VR platforms
