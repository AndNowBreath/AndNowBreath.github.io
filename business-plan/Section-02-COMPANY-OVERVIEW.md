## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Mission Statement

To make evidence-based mental health support accessible to everyone by removing barriers to effective meditation practice through AI-powered, personalized XR experiences.

Our mission recognizes that traditional guided meditation, while clinically proven, excludes a significant portion of people who struggle with visualization. By providing interactive 3D environments that users can see and manipulate, we democratize access to therapeutic techniques that support recovery, reduce anxiety, and improve mental wellbeing.

### 1.2 Vision

To become the leading digital therapeutic for attachment-based mental health challenges, integrated into NHS care pathways and recognized globally for combining clinical effectiveness with technological innovation.

Within five years, we envision And Now Breathe as:

- The **standard of care** for meditation-based interventions in addiction recovery services across the UK
- **Featured in NICE guidance** as a recommended digital therapeutic for anxiety and rumination
- **Prescribed by GPs** through social prescribing pathways, reaching 100,000+ users annually
- **Validated by clinical evidence** through peer-reviewed publications and randomized controlled trials
- **Expanded internationally** to English-speaking markets (Ireland, Canada, Australia) and beyond

### 1.3 Core Values

**Evidence-First**  
Clinical validation drives every product decision. We commit to rigorous research methodologies, transparent outcome reporting, and continuous improvement based on data. We will never make therapeutic claims without supporting evidence, and we actively partner with academic institutions to ensure scientific rigor.

**Accessibility**  
Mental health support should be available to everyone, regardless of device ownership or technical capability. Our graceful enhancement architecture ensures that the core therapeutic value is accessible on basic smartphones, while those with VR headsets experience enhanced immersion. We design for diverse abilities, languages, and cultural backgrounds.

**Privacy & Safety**  
Users trust us with sensitive mental health data during vulnerable moments. We protect this trust through encryption, data minimization, transparent privacy policies, and crisis intervention protocols. User wellbeing takes absolute priority over business metrics.

**Social Impact**  
As a Community Interest Company, we exist to serve those struggling with mental health challenges, particularly underserved populations in addiction recovery. While we aim for financial sustainability and growth, our success is measured primarily by lives improved and recovery supported.

**Innovation with Purpose**  
Technology serves therapeutic outcomes, not the reverse. We embrace cutting-edge AI and XR capabilities only when they demonstrably enhance clinical efficacy, user engagement, or accessibility. We avoid "innovation theater" and focus on meaningful improvements to mental health support.

### 1.4 Legal Structure

**Recommended Entity:** Community Interest Company (CIC) Limited by Shares

**Rationale for CIC Structure:**

A Community Interest Company is a special type of limited company designed for social enterprises that want to use their profits and assets for the public good. For And Now Breathe, this structure offers multiple strategic advantages:

1. **Grant Eligibility:** CICs are strongly favored by UK grant bodies including Innovate UK, NIHR, and regional innovation funds. Many social impact grants explicitly require or prefer CIC status. This could represent £200-500K in additional accessible funding.

2. **NHS Credibility:** NHS trusts and commissioners view CICs more favorably than standard limited companies for mental health services. The asset lock (explained below) demonstrates commitment to patient outcomes over profit maximization, aligning with NHS values.

3. **Mission Protection:** The CIC structure includes an "asset lock" that prevents the company and its assets from being used for purposes other than the community interest. This protects the mental health mission even if ownership changes or investors join. Assets cannot be distributed to shareholders except through specific permitted channels.

4. **Investor Compatibility:** Unlike charities, CICs can raise investment and distribute profits to shareholders, though with a cap (currently 35% of distributable profits for CICs with share capital). This allows us to attract impact investors while maintaining social purpose.

5. **Brand Differentiation:** The "CIC" designation signals to users, therapists, and commissioners that this is not a typical profit-driven tech company, but a mission-aligned organization. This builds trust, particularly important given the sensitive nature of mental health data.

6. **Regulatory Advantages:** CICs have lighter regulatory burden than charities (no Charity Commission registration) but stronger mission protection than standard companies. They file annual CIC34 reports demonstrating social impact, which also serves as marketing collateral for grants and NHS contracts.

**CIC Regulatory Requirements:**

- Annual CIC Report (CIC34) detailing social impact activities
- Community Interest Statement defining how the company serves the community
- Asset lock ensuring assets used for social purpose
- Dividend cap (35% of distributable profits maximum)
- Regulated by CIC Regulator (part of Companies House)

**Formation Timeline and Process:**

**Week 1:**

1. Choose company name: "And Now Breathe CIC" (check availability on Companies House)
2. Draft Community Interest Statement (3-4 paragraphs explaining how we serve mental health recovery)
3. Identify initial director(s) (founder initially, co-founders added later)
4. Prepare Articles of Association (can use model articles with CIC-specific provisions)

**Week 2:** 5. Register online via Companies House (£35 fee for CIC, slightly higher than standard £12 company registration) 6. Receive Certificate of Incorporation (typically within 24 hours for online applications) 7. Register for Corporation Tax with HMRC (within 3 months of starting business) 8. Open business bank account (many banks have CIC-specific accounts, e.g., Triodos, Unity Trust)

**Total Formation Cost:** £500-800

- Companies House registration: £35
- Registered office address (if not using home): £100-200/year (optional)
- Accountant/solicitor for Articles review: £300-500 (recommended but optional)

**Alternative Consideration:**

If unable to attract co-founders or investors comfortable with the CIC dividend cap, we could alternatively incorporate as a standard Limited Company and convert to CIC status later (though this is administratively complex). However, given our NHS-primary strategy, the grant opportunities, and mission alignment, CIC is strongly recommended from day one.

### 1.5 Founding Story

The genesis of And Now Breathe emerges from the intersection of personal experience, professional expertise, and identified market gap.

**Founder Background:**

The founder brings 20+ years of experience as a creative technologist, with deep expertise spanning multiple technology stacks, including web development, 3D graphics, interactive experiences, and emerging technologies. This career has included work across various sectors, consistently focused on using technology to solve meaningful human problems.

**The Problem Discovery:**

Through ongoing work with a regional recovery network developing smartphone applications for addiction support, the founder observed a recurring pattern: when recovery workers introduced mindfulness and meditation practices to clients, many struggled significantly with the visualization components. Traditional guided meditations instruct participants to "imagine a peaceful stream" or "visualize a balloon floating away," but for individuals experiencing:

- **Active addiction or early withdrawal:** Cognitive impairment makes visualization extremely difficult
- **Anxiety and rumination:** Racing thoughts prevent the mental stillness required for visualization
- **Aphantasia:** A neurological condition (affecting 2-5% of the population) making mental imagery impossible
- **Trauma responses:** Closing eyes and visualizing can trigger feelings of vulnerability or traumatic memories

These barriers meant that evidence-based therapeutic techniques like "leaves on a stream" (from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy) were inaccessible to those who needed them most.

**The Insight:**

What if technology could externalize the visualization? Instead of requiring users to imagine a stream with leaves floating past, what if they could actually _see_ it, _interact_ with it, and _personalize_ it? This wouldn't replace traditional meditation for those who can visualize effectively, but it would open access to a significant underserved population.

**Technical Validation:**

Leveraging expertise in Three.js (3D graphics library), physics engines, and emerging AI technologies (particularly large language models and text-to-speech), the founder built initial prototypes demonstrating:

- Realistic 3D environments (forest stream, ocean beach) rendered in web browsers
- Physics-based object behavior (leaves floating naturally down stream)
- User interaction (typing thoughts onto objects via virtual keyboard)
- AI narrative integration (LLM processing user inputs and generating personalized meditation narration)
- Voice synthesis (converting AI-generated text to natural-sounding speech)

**Market Validation:**

Discussions with recovery network colleagues revealed:

1. **Strong clinical interest:** Therapists and counselors immediately recognized the value for their clients
2. **Limited competition:** No existing solutions specifically addressed visualization barriers with this combination of AI personalization and device accessibility
3. **NHS alignment:** Digital therapeutics are a priority area for NHS investment, particularly for mental health and addiction
4. **Grant opportunities:** Multiple UK funding programs support mental health innovation with clear commercialization pathways

**The Decision:**

These factors converged into the decision to pursue And Now Breathe as a focused venture. The combination of technical feasibility (proven prototypes), clinical need (observed directly in target population), market opportunity (growing digital health budgets), and personal mission (improving mental health access) created a compelling case for dedicated development.

**Current Status:**

The founder has invested significant personal time developing working prototypes of all core technical components, establishing relationships with potential pilot sites, and researching grant opportunities and regulatory pathways. The business plan you're reading represents the transition from concept validation to structured commercialization.

### 1.6 Social Impact Commitment

As a CIC, And Now Breathe is legally bound to serve the community interest. Our social impact commitments include:

**Primary Beneficiaries:**

- Individuals in addiction recovery seeking accessible mental health support
- People with anxiety, depression, or rumination challenges unable to access traditional meditation
- NHS patients waiting for therapy (6-8 week average IAPT waiting times)
- Individuals with aphantasia or other visualization difficulties

**Impact Metrics We Will Track:**

- Number of users supported (total reach)
- Engagement rates (sessions completed, retention)
- Clinical outcomes (PHQ-9, GAD-7 score reductions where measured)
- Accessibility (% of users on basic devices vs. premium VR)
- Economic impact (NHS cost savings through reduced GP visits, crisis interventions)

**Commitment to Accessibility:**

- Free tier always available (never fully paywalled)
- Pricing tiers ensure affordability across income levels
- Graceful enhancement ensures basic smartphones provide core therapeutic value
- Scholarship program (Year 2+) for individuals unable to afford premium subscriptions

**Commitment to Evidence:**

- All research findings published openly (peer-reviewed journals, preprint servers)
- Negative or null results disclosed transparently (not hidden)
- Independent evaluation welcomed (third-party outcome assessments)
- User data made available (anonymized) for academic research advancing mental health understanding

**Commitment to Privacy:**

- User data never sold to third parties
- Minimal data collection (only what's necessary for service delivery)
- Users control their data (view, export, delete at any time)
- Transparent privacy policy in plain English

**Annual CIC Report:**
Each year, we will publish a comprehensive Community Interest Report detailing:

- Social impact achieved (users served, outcomes measured)
- Financial performance (revenue, costs, profit distribution)
- Governance (board decisions, stakeholder consultation)
- Future plans (how profits will be reinvested in the mission)

This report will be public and submitted to the CIC Regulator as required by law.
