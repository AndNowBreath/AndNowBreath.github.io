# SECTION 7: GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY - COMPLETE VERSION

## 7.1 Go-to-Market Overview

### 7.1.1 Phased Market Entry Strategy

Our go-to-market approach follows a deliberate, evidence-driven sequence that builds credibility before scaling.

**Phase 1: Foundation (Months 0-12) - "Prove It Works"**

- **Focus:** NHS addiction recovery services (beachhead market)
- **Goal:** Clinical evidence, testimonials, 2 paying customers
- **Revenue Target:** £12K
- **Key Activities:** Beta testing, pilot study, grant applications, MVP launch

**Phase 2: Credibility (Months 12-24) - "Scale What Works"**

- **Focus:** Expand NHS, launch corporate pilots, soft-launch consumer
- **Goal:** NHS Apps Library listing, peer-reviewed publication, 20 customers
- **Revenue Target:** £625K
- **Key Activities:** Evidence dissemination, sales team hire, corporate pilots

**Phase 3: Scale (Months 24-36) - "Go National"**

- **Focus:** National NHS presence, corporate expansion, consumer public launch
- **Goal:** 85 customers across segments, recognized brand
- **Revenue Target:** £1.94M
- **Key Activities:** RCT launch, international pilots, team scaling

---

### 7.1.2 Market Entry Principles

**1. Evidence Before Scale**

- Don't mass-market until we have proof it works (pilot data minimum)
- NHS Apps Library listing before major NHS sales push
- Peer-reviewed publication before international expansion

**2. Beachhead Strategy**

- Start with addiction recovery (focused, underserved, we can win)
- Prove model there before expanding to broader mental health (IAPT, general anxiety)
- Geographic focus (East Midlands Year 1, national Year 2-3)

**3. Channel Sequencing**

- **Year 1:** NHS only (build foundation, gather evidence)
- **Year 2:** Add corporate (faster revenue, diversification)
- **Year 3:** Add consumer at scale (brand awareness, long-term volume)

**4. Relationship Over Transaction**

- B2B sales are relationship-driven (6-12 month cycles, trust-building)
- Invest in customer success (renewals more valuable than new logos)
- Community building (user groups, practitioner networks)

**5. Capital Efficiency**

- Bootstrap with grants + customer revenue (minimize equity dilution)
- Organic growth prioritized over paid acquisition where possible
- Measure CAC:LTV religiously (pivot if economics don't work)

### 7.2.3 Sales Process & Conversion

**Stage 1: Discovery Call (30 minutes)**

**Objectives:**

- Understand needs, pain points, current meditation approaches
- Qualify: Budget authority? Timeline? Fit with ICP?
- Build rapport

**Structure:**

- **5 min:** Founder intro (brief background, why we built this)
- **10 min:** Ask questions:
  - "How many clients in treatment?"
  - "Do you currently use meditation or mindfulness approaches?"
  - "What barriers to client engagement with meditation?"
  - "How do you measure outcomes? (PHQ-9, GAD-7?)"
  - "Process for adopting new digital tools?"
- **10 min:** Share approach
  - Show 2-minute video demo
  - Share pilot results slide
- **5 min:** Next steps
  - "Seems like good fit. Next step: detailed demo with clinical team. Does [date] work?"

**Outcome:** 70% of discovery calls lead to demo meeting

---

**Stage 2: Product Demo (45-60 minutes)**

**Attendees:** Clinical lead, 1-2 frontline staff, possibly service manager

**Objectives:**

- Show product (they experience it, not just watch)
- Address clinical questions
- Identify objections early
- Secure frontline staff buy-in

**Structure:**

- **10 min:** Context-setting
- **20 min:** Guided hands-on demo
  - Everyone downloads app or uses tablets
  - Founder guides: "Let's try leaves on a stream together..."
  - Participants type thought, release leaf, experience AI personalization
  - **Key moment:** AI says their specific concern back to them
- **15 min:** Evidence & outcomes
  - Present pilot results slides
  - Share clinical foundation (ACT, cognitive defusion)
  - Discuss safety (crisis detection, data protection)
- **10 min:** Implementation discussion
  - "How would you introduce to clients?"
  - "What outcome measures matter?"
  - "What concerns?"
- **5 min:** Proposal & next steps

**Outcome:** 60% of demos lead to proposal review

---

**Stage 3: Proposal & Negotiation (2-4 weeks)**

**Pilot Proposal Contents:**

1. Summary: 3-month pilot, 30-50 clients, £3K (50% discount)
2. What's included: App access, training, reports, evaluation
3. Success metrics (co-defined):
   - Engagement: 40%+ complete 3+ sessions
   - Satisfaction: 70%+ report helpful
   - Outcomes: Significant GAD-7/PHQ-9 reduction
4. Timeline: Week 1 training, Weeks 2-12 use, Week 13 evaluation
5. Investment: £3,000
6. Next steps: Review, confirm, schedule kickoff

**Common Objections & Responses:**

**"We don't have budget this year."**
"Understood. Many services use innovation funds or digital transformation budgets. Also, we have grant funding that could offset cost. What if we found way to pilot at no cost?"

**"Our clients don't have smartphones."**
"We've found 80-90% do, but we can provide tablets for those who don't. Would lending 5-10 tablets remove this barrier?"

**"We're already using Headspace."**
"Great! Headspace is excellent for people who can visualize. We're hearing 30-40% of clients struggle with visualization-based meditation. We'd be complementary."

**"We need to see more evidence."**
"Completely fair. Let me send pilot study results. Also happy to connect you with [Reference Customer]. What specific evidence would help?"

**Outcome:** 50% of proposals lead to signed pilots

---

**Stage 4: Pilot Execution & Evaluation (3 months)**

**Onboarding (Week 1):**

- Day 1: Welcome email with access codes
- Day 3: Live training webinar (1 hour)
- Day 5: Check-in email

**Ongoing (Weeks 2-12):**

- Weekly: Automated engagement reports
- Monthly: Customer success call
- Flags: Crisis protocol triggers → Notify service

**Evaluation (Week 13):**

- Present results: Engagement, outcomes, testimonials
- Conversion discussion: Pilot → Full contract

**Outcome:** 70% of successful pilots convert to full contracts

## 7.9 GTM Risks & Mitigation

**Risk 1: NHS Procurement Delays**

- **Impact:** Revenue shifts right
- **Mitigation:** Diversify to corporate early, 3x pipeline, pilot programs, grants fund operations

**Risk 2: Consumer CAC Too High**

- **Impact:** Unprofitable consumer segment
- **Mitigation:** Organic-first, conservative paid ads, LTV improvement, pricing experiments

**Risk 3: Competitors Copy**

- **Impact:** Differentiation erodes
- **Mitigation:** Evidence moat (2-3 years ahead), NHS relationships, continuous innovation, niche focus

**Risk 4: Messaging Doesn't Resonate**

- **Impact:** Low conversion rates
- **Mitigation:** User research, A/B testing, segment-specific messaging, video over text

**Risk 5: Team Bandwidth**

- **Impact:** Burnout, poor quality
- **Mitigation:** Prioritize ruthlessly, hire ahead, automate, co-founder recruitment, advisors
